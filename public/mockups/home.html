Built for the border record.

The GOAT Initiative gathers intelligence along the U.S.–Mexico border and sends it to law enforcement. Child trafficking and human trafficking are a central part of that work. The team identifies what it sees, documents it, and reports it. It does not arrest, detain, or take custody.
